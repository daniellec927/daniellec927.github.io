A structure that works well for data science:

1. **Problem** — what question you were answering, and for whom
2. **Data** — source, size, how you got it, what was messy about it
3. **Approach** — the method, and why that one
4. **Results** — a number, a chart, or a decision it enabled
5. **Links** — repo, notebook, dashboard, write-up

Formatting snippets live in the example blog posts (`_posts/`). The most useful ones here are
`images`, `code`, `diagrams`, `tables`, and `jupyter-notebook`.
